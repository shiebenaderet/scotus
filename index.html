The driving question

When, if ever, should the government be allowed to limit a person's constitutional rights in order to protect the community?

In other words: When should the government be able to take away someone's rights to keep other people safe?

This is the unit’s big question, and it fits some cases better than others. Jacobson fits it most directly: one man’s liberty against a city fighting smallpox. Tinker, T.L.O., Payton, Mahanoy, and Morse are also genuine rights-versus-order fights. Kennedy is two religion clauses colliding. Brown is equal protection. Arizona is federal versus state power. Fishing Rights asks what the treaties promised the tribes and whether a treaty outranks state law. Use this question when it fits, and use each case’s own constitutional question when it does not.
